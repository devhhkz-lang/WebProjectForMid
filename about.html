About Foodly

Foodly is a student web-programming project: an online food ordering website with a menu, cart, checkout, user accounts and an admin panel. It works entirely in your browser using localStorage.

Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Usually 30–45 minutes, depending on the dish and the distance.

What does delivery cost?

590 ₸ per order. Orders from 8 000 ₸ are delivered for free.

Is there a promo code?

Yes — use WELCOME10 in the cart to get 10% off.

Can I cancel an order?

You can cancel an order from your profile while its status is "Pending".

Privacy

We only store the data needed to run your account and orders. In this demo everything stays in your own browser and is never sent to a server.
